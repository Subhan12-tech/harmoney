MERIDIAN SYSTEMS INC. - Q2 FY2026 EARNINGS CALL TRANSCRIPT (fictional sample)
Date: 16 July 2026 | Speakers: Dana Okafor (CEO), Priyanka Raman (CFO)

DANA OKAFOR: Second-quarter revenue was $124.7 million, up 7% year over year.
We closed the quarter with 1,240 enterprise customers, of which 78 now generate
more than $1 million in annual recurring revenue.

Atlas Sync reached general availability in May and is already deployed at 60
customers.

PRIYANKA RAMAN: Gross margin was 71.2%, down 30 basis points sequentially,
reflecting the infrastructure investment behind Atlas Sync.

Net revenue retention moderated to 112%. We want to be direct that this is a
deceleration from the 114% we reported in the first quarter, driven by smaller
expansion deals in our mid-market cohort rather than by increased churn. Gross
logo churn was stable at 6.2% on an annualised basis.

Headcount was 2,890 at quarter end, down from 2,940, reflecting the reduction we
announced in March rather than attrition.

We are reaffirming full-year guidance of high single-digit growth, $478 million
to $486 million.

Free cash flow was negative $4.1 million in the quarter.
