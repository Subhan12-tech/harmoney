MERIDIAN SYSTEMS INC. - Q3 FY2026 EARNINGS CALL TRANSCRIPT (fictional sample)
Date: 15 October 2026 | Speakers: Dana Okafor (CEO), Priyanka Raman (CFO)

DANA OKAFOR: Third-quarter revenue came in at $131.2 million, up 8% year over
year. We ended the quarter with 1,310 enterprise customers, 84 of which exceed
$1 million in annual recurring revenue.

PRIYANKA RAMAN: This was a milestone quarter on two fronts. Operating margin was
positive 4.1%, our first positive operating quarter as a public company, and free
cash flow was positive $12.3 million, also a first.

Gross margin was 70.8%. Net revenue retention was 109%, a further moderation from
112% in the second quarter. We expect retention to stabilise in this range rather
than recover to prior levels in the near term.

Headcount closed at 2,850. Research and development represented 22% of revenue.

Our largest customer accounted for 4.8% of revenue. Cash stood at $284 million
with no debt outstanding. Under our $50 million buyback authorisation we have
repurchased $18 million to date.

We are reaffirming full-year guidance of high single-digit growth, $478 million
to $486 million.
