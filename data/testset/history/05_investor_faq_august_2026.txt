MERIDIAN SYSTEMS INC. - INVESTOR FAQ (fictional sample)
Published: 3 August 2026

Q: How does Meridian charge for its products?
A: Meridian has operated a consumption-based pricing model since FY2025, when we
moved away from per-seat licensing. Customers are billed on data volume
processed.

Q: What is your customer concentration risk?
A: As of the second quarter of FY2026, our largest single customer represented
5.0% of revenue. No other customer exceeds 3%.

Q: Is Meridian profitable?
A: Not yet on an operating basis. We were operating-margin negative in the first
half of FY2026 and expect our first positive operating quarter before the end of
the fiscal year.

Q: Where is customer data stored?
A: In the region the customer selects: Virginia or Oregon in the United States,
or Frankfurt in the European Union. We do not currently operate an Asia-Pacific
region.

Q: How many people does Meridian employ?
A: Approximately 2,890 as of the end of the second quarter, of whom roughly 400
are based in the European Union.
