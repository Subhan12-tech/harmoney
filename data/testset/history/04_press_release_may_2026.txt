MERIDIAN SYSTEMS ANNOUNCES GENERAL AVAILABILITY OF ATLAS SYNC

Meridian Systems Inc. today announced the general availability of Atlas Sync, its
cross-region data replication service.

Atlas Sync is available in the existing Meridian regions: Virginia and Oregon in
the United States, and Frankfurt in the European Union. An Asia-Pacific region
remains under evaluation and has not been scheduled.

"Atlas Sync is the product our largest customers have asked for," said Dana
Okafor, Chief Executive Officer.

Meridian achieved SOC 2 Type II certification in March 2026. The ISO 27001
certification effort is in progress and has not yet been completed.
