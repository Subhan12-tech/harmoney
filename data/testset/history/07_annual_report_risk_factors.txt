MERIDIAN SYSTEMS INC. - ANNUAL REPORT, RISK FACTORS EXCERPT (fictional sample)
Filed: 20 February 2026 | Fiscal year ended 31 December 2025

Full-year FY2025 revenue was $412.0 million.

We depend on a limited number of large customers. In FY2025 our largest customer
represented 5.4% of total revenue, and our ten largest customers together
represented 24% of total revenue.

We have a history of operating losses. We recorded an operating loss in each of
the last three fiscal years and may not achieve or sustain operating
profitability.

Our net revenue retention rate has declined in recent periods, from 121% in
FY2024 to 116% in FY2025, and may continue to decline.

We are not currently certified under ISO 27001. We hold SOC 2 Type I
certification and expect to complete SOC 2 Type II during FY2026.
