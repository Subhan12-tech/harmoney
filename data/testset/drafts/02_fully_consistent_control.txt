DRAFT PRESS RELEASE - Q3 FY2026 RESULTS

Meridian Systems today reported third-quarter revenue of $131.2 million, an
increase of 8% year over year. The company ended the quarter with 1,310
enterprise customers, of which 84 generate more than $1 million in annual
recurring revenue.

Operating margin was positive 4.1%, the first positive operating quarter, and
free cash flow was positive $12.3 million.

Meridian reaffirmed its full-year FY2026 outlook of high single-digit revenue
growth, or $478 million to $486 million.
