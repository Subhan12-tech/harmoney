DRAFT PRESS RELEASE - Q3 FY2026 RESULTS

Meridian Systems today announced third-quarter revenue of $131.2 million,
representing double-digit year-over-year growth and continued acceleration in
our enterprise business.

The company ended the quarter serving more than 1,500 enterprise customers, and
net revenue retention improved to 118%.

Meridian remains on track to deliver full-year revenue growth in the low teens.
